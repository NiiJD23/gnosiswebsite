What it does

An assistant that already knows your world.

Chats that remember

Keep a separate conversation for every topic. Rename, pin, search and clear them. Say “remember that…” and it is saved on the spot.

One memory for everything

Add notes, documents and web links. Gnosis reads and indexes them on your computer, and you can edit or delete any of it.

Answers with sources

Search combines exact words with meaning, so it finds the right passage. Each reply points to the memory it used.

Suggestions, never surprises

When Gnosis notices a fact worth keeping, it puts it in an inbox. You accept it or dismiss it. Nothing is memorised silently.

Advice in your style

Tell it who you are, what matters and how you like to be advised: as a coach, a risk register, or plain and brief.

Move to a new laptop

One password-protected backup file carries your memory, chats and settings to another machine.

Privacy

The cloud only sees what you let it see.

Everything runs on your computer by default. If you want a stronger model for one question, you can switch the cloud on for that message. Gnosis swaps personal details for placeholders first, shows you exactly what will be sent, and puts your details back into the reply.

  • The cloud is off until you turn it on.
  • You review a preview before anything is sent.
  • Names you list, emails, phone numbers, postcodes and card numbers are replaced by placeholders.
  • Your cloud key lives in your system keychain, not in a file.

How it works

From question to answer, step by step.

  1. 1

    You ask

    Type a question in plain language, in any of your chats.

  2. 2

    It looks

    Gnosis searches your notes, files, links and past chats by keyword and by meaning, on your computer.

  3. 3

    It answers

    A model running on your machine writes the reply and marks its sources 1 so you can open them.

  4. 4

    You decide

    New facts it noticed wait in your inbox. Keep the ones you want.

Mac app

Gnosis as an app with its own icon. Unzip it and drag it to Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open.

Mac and Windows

The full package. On a Mac, double-click start.command. On Windows, double-click start.bat. Your browser opens Gnosis.

Download the package

Before you start: install Ollama and open it once. It runs the models on your computer.

If a model is missing, Gnosis shows an Install button and downloads it for you. The chat model is about 5 GB and the memory model about 270 MB, once only.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to download the models the first time, and only if you choose to use the cloud option. Chatting, searching and remembering all work on your computer.

Where is my data kept?

In a private folder on your computer, outside Documents, OneDrive and iCloud so syncing cannot damage it. Nothing is stored on a Gnosis server.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for.

Which models does it use?

By default, Llama 3.1 8B for chat and nomic-embed-text for memory, both through Ollama. You can pick other installed models in Settings.

What can I add to memory?

Notes you type, files such as PDF and Word documents, and web links. You can also add a fact from a chat by saying “remember that…”.

What if I want to move to another computer?

Create a password-protected backup in Settings and restore it on the other machine.
