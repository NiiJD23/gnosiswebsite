Privacy notice

How this website handles your data.

Last updated 17 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Promintal Consulting Limited. We are the controller of the data described here. Contact us at info@promintal.co.uk.

What we collect

We count visits so we can see which pages are useful and how many people download Gnosis. For each visit we record:

  • which page was opened, and the website you came from, if any;
  • your country and city, worked out from your connection by our host, and whether you use a desktop, tablet or phone;
  • a random visitor ID stored in your browser, so we can tell returning visitors from new ones;
  • whether you clicked a download button, used the example chat on the Product page, or prepared a demo or contact message.

We do not store your IP address, your name or your email address. We do not store anything you type into the example chat or into the forms. The forms only prepare an email draft in your browser, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

Your choice

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we do not record your visit at all. You can also clear your browser data at any time, which removes the visitor ID.

Why we use it

To understand and improve the website and Gnosis. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving our service.

Who processes it

The website and its database are hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes the data on our behalf. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Visit records are deleted automatically after about 13 months.

Gnosis the app

The Gnosis app itself does not send usage data to us. It runs on your computer and keeps your memory there.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete data about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@promintal.co.uk. Because we do not store names or emails, we may need your visitor ID to find your records. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
