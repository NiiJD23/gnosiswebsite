Privacy

The cloud only sees what you let it see.

Everything runs on your computer by default. If you want a stronger model for one question, you can switch the cloud on for that message. Gnosis swaps personal details for placeholders first, shows you exactly what will be sent, and puts your details back into the reply.

  • The cloud is off until you turn it on.
  • You review a preview before anything is sent.
  • Your cloud key lives in your system keychain, not in a file.

How it works

From question to answer, step by step.

  1. 1

    You ask

    Type a question in plain language, in any of your chats.

  2. 2

    It looks

    Gnosis searches your notes, files, links and past chats by keyword and by meaning, on your computer.

  3. 3

    It answers

    A model running on your machine writes the reply and marks its sources 1 so you can open them.

  4. 4

    You decide

    New facts it noticed wait in your inbox. Keep the ones you want.
